Symbol: $symbol

PROPOSED SETUP:
$setup_data

ACCOUNT RISK PARAMETERS:
$risk_parameters

Assess:
1. Is the stop-loss distance sensible given the current ATR/volatility?
2. Is the risk/reward ratio acceptable under the stated parameters?
3. Is the suggested leverage safe for this volatility level?
4. What is the realistic worst-case outcome of this trade?
5. Should this setup be reduced in size, or skipped entirely?

Be conservative. Capital preservation outranks profit potential. If the setup is unsound, say so plainly.
